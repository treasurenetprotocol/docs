## 1. Data Controller
Treasurenet is a blockchain project centered around open-source protocols and decentralized infrastructure. The official website, documentation, and related front-end services of Treasurenet are maintained and operated by the project maintainers.
Under applicable data protection laws (such as the GDPR), data processing activities related to Treasurenet’s website and front-end services are carried out by the relevant operating or maintaining entity acting as the Data Controller.

## 2. Scope of This Privacy Policy
This Privacy Policy applies to:
- The official Treasurenet website and its subdomains
- Treasurenet documentation (Docs) and developer resources
- Front-end services directly associated with the above websites

This Policy does not apply to:
- Transactions, wallet addresses, or smart contract data on public blockchains
- Data collected by third-party websites, wallets, blockchain explorers, or infrastructure providers

## 3. Information Collected Automatically
When you visit Treasurenet websites, servers may automatically record certain technical information, including:
- Date and time of access and requested pages
- Browser type and version
- Operating system type
- IP addresses that are anonymized or truncated where applicable

This information is used solely for:
- Ensuring website security and stable operation
- Preventing abuse and malicious activity
- System maintenance and troubleshooting

Such data is not used to identify individual users.

## 4. Cookies and Analytics
Treasurenet may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes including:
- Storing basic functional settings (such as language preferences)
- Understanding aggregated website usage to improve content and structure

Where analytics tools are used, we strive to adopt privacy-friendly analytics solutions (for example, anonymized statistics) and provide users with reasonable choices.
You may manage or disable cookies through your browser settings.

## 5. Information You Voluntarily Provide
You may voluntarily provide information to Treasurenet in the following situations:
- Contacting us via contact forms or email
- Submitting Issues or Pull Requests on GitHub
- Participating in community discussions or providing feedback

Such information is used solely for communication, collaboration, and issue resolution, and is not used for marketing or profiling purposes.

## 6. Blockchain and On-Chain Data Disclaimer
Treasurenet is built on public blockchain networks. Core characteristics of blockchains include:
- Publicly accessible transaction and address data
- Immutability of data once recorded on-chain

On-chain data is not created, controlled, or stored by Treasurenet and therefore falls outside the scope of this Privacy Policy.

## 7. Use and Sharing of Data
Treasurenet does not sell, rent, or otherwise commercialize user data.
Where necessary, limited data may be shared with:
- Technical service providers offering website hosting, infrastructure, or security services
- Government authorities or regulators where required by applicable laws or regulations

Any such sharing is limited to what is necessary for legitimate and lawful purposes.

## 8. Data Security
We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data security, including:
- Encrypted communications (HTTPS / TLS)
- Restricted access controls
- Adherence to the principle of least privilege

However, no method of transmission or storage over the internet can be guaranteed to be absolutely secure.

## 9. Your Rights
To the extent permitted by applicable law, you have the right to:
- Request information about how your data is processed
- Request access to, correction, or deletion of personal data you have voluntarily provided
- Object to or restrict certain data processing activities

Due to the immutable nature of blockchains, on-chain data generally cannot be modified or deleted.

## 10. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect technical developments, legal requirements, or operational needs.
Updated versions will be published on the official website or documentation, together with the effective date.

## 11. Contact Us
If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact us through:
- The official Treasurenet GitHub repositories (Issues)
- Official community channels
- Email: contact@treasurenet.org
